# FTMO 2‑Step Challenge – $10,000 Account

- **Phase 1 (Challenge)**: 10 % profit target.
- **Phase 2 (Verification)**: 5 % profit target.
- **Maximum daily loss**: 5 % of starting balance ($500). Includes closed trades, floating P&L, commissions, swaps.
- **Maximum overall loss**: 10 % of starting balance ($1,000). Equity must never drop below $9,000.
- **Minimum trading days**: 4 days per phase; no maximum time limit.
- **Fee**: $89, fully refundable after passing both phases.

All loss limits reset at midnight CET for daily loss; overall loss is cumulative.
Stay below 3 % daily loss to keep a safety buffer.
